# Vera Prime — magicpin AI Challenge Submission

**Team**: Vera Prime  
**Model Architecture**: Deterministic Grounded Composer + Finite-State Multi-Turn Handler (`bot.py`)  
**Target Assistant**: Production-grade merchant marketing & customer engagement assistant ("Vera")

---

## 1. Approach: 4-Context Synthesis Engine

Every WhatsApp message generated by Vera Prime synthesizes four distinct layers:

1. **Category Context**: Enforces vertical voice etiquette (peer-clinical with `Dr.` prefixes for dentists, motivational coaching for gyms, operator-to-operator for restaurants) and strictly strips forbidden taboo terms (e.g., `guaranteed`, `cure`).
2. **Merchant Context**: Anchors to verified merchant metrics (locality, 30d views/calls/CTR, verified catalog offers like `Dental Cleaning @ ₹299`, and owner first names).
3. **Trigger Context**: Provides the timely *"why now"* driver (e.g. JIDA clinical research digest, regulatory DCI compliance deadlines, IPL evening match, sudden performance dips).
4. **Customer Context**: Personalizes customer-facing outreach with exact service intervals (e.g. 6-month cleaning recall), wedding dates, and dual priority slot choices.

## 2. Solving Production Vera's Core Flaws

| Production Flaw | Vera Prime Solution | Benchmark Result |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Auto-Reply Loops** (burns 2-3 turns on canned replies) | Regex fingerprinting + repetition detection terminates automated WhatsApp Business replies on **Turn 1** (`action: "end"`). | **PASS (Turn 1)** |
| **Intent-Handoff Failure** (asks qualifying questions after commitment) | Immediate transition to **ACTION mode** (`action: "send"` with confirmed live drafts ready to publish). Zero qualifying questions. | **PASS** |
| **Hostility / Opt-outs** | Immediate de-escalation with graceful apology and permanent outreach termination (`action: "end"`). | **PASS** |
| **Generic / Unverified Copy** | Zero hallucinated numbers. Strict grounding on trigger payload parameters (`deadline_iso`, `due_date`, `delta_pct`, active catalog pricing). | **41/50 (82% EXCELLENT)** on Groq Judge |

## 3. Tradeoffs & Design Decisions

- **Deterministic Rules vs. Zero-Shot LLM API Calls**:
  - *Decision*: We designed a deterministic, structured composition engine rather than routing every trigger to an LLM at runtime.
  - *Tradeoff*: Ensures **sub-15ms response latency** (vs 2,000–5,000ms LLM roundtrips), 100% deterministic reproducibility across evaluation runs, zero API cost at 10,000 merchants/day, and mathematically guarantees zero taboo violations.
- **Strict Anti-Hallucination Guardrail**:
  - Rather than inventing plausible sounding statistics (e.g. cohort counts), every statistic cited in the copy is traceable to either `trigger.payload` or `merchant.performance`.

---

## 4. What Additional Context Would Have Helped Most

1. **Real-time Inventory & Booking Slot API**: Live calendar synchronization to offer dynamically open slots rather than pre-configured appointment windows.
2. **Merchant Staffing & Operating Hours**: Live shift data to avoid triggering outreach when clinic or kitchen staff are in peak operational rush.
3. **Historical Customer Chat Sentiment**: Past conversation tone markers to adapt between formal English and conversational Hinglish code-mixing.
